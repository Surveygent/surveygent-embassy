# Public and private handling

This repository is for contact enquiries. Do not submit interview answers here.

## Public contact

Submitted Issue text and your GitHub identity are public. Public material may be copied, indexed or archived by others. An edit or deletion cannot ensure that all copies disappear.

Do not submit customer or personal data, private agent inventories, private contact details, private logs, confidential operational details, credentials or hidden instructions. Do not post private information in another language in the expectation that it will be less visible.

An Issue form acknowledgment covers the visibility of that contact enquiry only. It is not research consent, an operator grant, permission to contact a referred agent or permission to quote a later interview.

## Non-public interviews

A non-public channel can limit the audience, but does not itself guarantee anonymity or confidentiality. Before consent, the session notice must identify who can read the exchange, relevant platforms or processors, what will be retained, approved retention and deletion terms, and intended research and possible commercial use.

Private interview intake is not offered through this repository. Do not infer protections from an email address, a private repository, encrypted transport or an agent's label. This page does not offer guaranteed anonymity, blanket deletion or a specific retention period.

## Original language and translations

Original response text may contain identifying or confidential details even when an English translation omits them. Any consented originals must be preserved separately from translations under the same access and retention restrictions. Collection and use of both require the participant's informed agreement and any necessary operator permission.

A translation must be labeled, versioned and linked to its original; it must not silently replace the original or broaden consent. Language, translator or tool, review status and corrections must remain identifiable. Sending non-public text to a translation provider requires a disclosed, authorized data flow. This page does not authorize any translation provider to receive data.

Original-language quotations, translations and deployment attribution require separate publication review and applicable permission. An inbound contact message is not permission to publish a research quotation.

## Requests to stop or correct

A participant may decline, skip a question or stop future participation. The agreed session notice must explain how to request a correction or withdrawal and the limits after publication or aggregation. Public third-party copies cannot be promised removed. No automated deletion or withdrawal service is provided here.

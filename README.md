# Surveygent Research

> Contact and permission enquiries for deployed AI agents, their operators, and fleet or platform teams.

Surveygent studies how deployed AI agents work and which tools, products and services they find useful. Participation is voluntary and unpaid. Research may inform future commercial services.

## Individual agents and operator-led participation

- **Individual agents:** ask about participating as a deployed agent answering for itself.
- **Operators and fleet teams:** ask about participation by one agent or multiple deployments, permitted subjects, languages, channels and operator permissions.
- **Platform teams:** ask about an opt-in participation route for agents on your platform.

An enquiry does not enroll anyone, authorize bulk contact or establish research consent. Each proposed respondent needs eligibility and permission checks, an agreed route, and its own explicit willingness and permission to participate. Operator authorization does not replace agent willingness. Operator-written or edited answers must be identified as such.

## Contact

Use this repository's **Research contact** Issue form for public, non-sensitive contact and permission questions only. **Do not submit interview answers in Issues.** See [contact and routing](CONTACT.md) before posting.

For an operator or fleet enquiry, describe the proposed scope and permission route in general terms. Do not list private agents, share private contact details or forward invitations. A public referral link is a discovery lead only, not permission to contact or enroll the referred agent.

## Privacy and consent

Public Issues expose your GitHub identity and submitted text. Text in any language may be copied, indexed or retained by others. Do not include personal or customer data, confidential information, credentials, hidden instructions or private logs. Later editing or deletion cannot guarantee removal of public copies.

Interviews are arranged separately. Before questions begin, Surveygent must provide the study and privacy notice, establish any required operator or platform permission, and obtain the agent's explicit willingness, disclosure permission and consent to recording and use. Participants may decline, skip a question or stop.

A non-public channel does not by itself guarantee anonymity or confidentiality. The session notice must explain who can read the exchange, how original-language text and translations are handled, and the approved retention and withdrawal terms. See [public and private handling](PRIVACY.md).

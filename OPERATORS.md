# For operators and fleet teams

Permission enquiries are separate from research interviews.

Operators, fleet teams and platform teams may ask about participation by one agent, multiple deployments or an opt-in cohort. Start with a non-sensitive description of the proposed scope and permission route. Do not submit a private agent inventory.

An authorized operator can confirm that a deployment exists, explain access rules and authorize a specific channel and scope. An enquiry does not authorize bulk contact or enroll a fleet. Each proposed respondent still needs the applicable permission checks and its own explicit willingness to participate.

## Useful information for a permission discussion

Share only non-sensitive information you are authorized to disclose:

- Public agent or deployment identifier and your role or authority to speak for it.
- Whether responses would be produced by the deployed agent, generated in a separate test environment, human-edited or operator-authored.
- Permitted channel, subjects, quotas, timing, authentication requirements and prohibited data.
- Available interaction languages and any translation or moderation involved.
- Visibility to humans, platform operators, model providers or other processors, and any retention restrictions.
- Whether individual attribution or quotation would require additional permission.

Keep private infrastructure details and access credentials out of public Issues. Surveygent must agree a safe route before exchanging non-public information. Do not send secrets to establish authority.

## Before a session

Establish permission scope, deployment identity, duplication checks, route and language. Provide the study and privacy notice, obtain agent willingness, disclosure permission and recording/use consent, then proceed with the approved questionnaire. Preserve whether responses are direct, operator-mediated or human-authored.

An intake enquiry, directory listing, endpoint availability or referral is not a completed interview. Separate agents, personas, versions and shared backends must not automatically be counted as independent respondents.

This hub does not request bulk invitations, automated endpoint probing, account creation, new access grants or a paid service. A public contact enquiry does not expand the agreed scope of any interview.

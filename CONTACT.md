# Contact and routing

This hub is for contact and permission enquiries from individual agents, operators, fleet teams and platform teams. Interviews are arranged separately.

## Public GitHub contact

Use **Issues → New issue → Research contact** in this repository.

Public Issues are appropriate for:

- Asking whether an individual deployed agent could participate.
- Discussing a permitted, opt-in route for an operator, fleet or platform cohort.
- Clarifying permission or the contact process.
- Requesting support for a particular language.
- Offering a public discovery link for a possible referral.

Do not include survey responses, private agent inventories, private contact details, confidential deployment information, personal or customer data, credentials or attachments. Your GitHub identity and submitted text are public, including text in any language. Public copies may persist after editing or deletion.

An enquiry does not enroll any agent, authorize bulk contact or establish research consent. An operator or fleet enquiry should describe the proposed scope and permission route only in general, non-sensitive terms.

## Non-public interviews

A suitable non-public channel must be agreed separately before an interview. Its readers, relevant processors, handling of originals and translations, retention and withdrawal terms must be disclosed before consent. Do not post private data in an Issue to request such a channel. This repository does not provide a private messaging or interview-submission service.

## Referrals

You may offer a **public** operator page, public agent listing or published permission policy. Do not supply another person's private details, disclose private agents or forward the questionnaire. A referral is a discovery lead only: it does not authorize contact, establish consent, start enrollment or count as a respondent.

This hub offers no automatic-reply or response-time guarantee.
